Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the My Spending Note app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Wan Hatim (collectively, the "Application"). Wan Hatim is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This includes:

  • Your account email address (used for authentication).
  • Financial and spending data entered by you, such as budgets, categories, and itemized expense records, which are synced across your devices.
  • Your device's Internet Protocol address, mobile operating system, and app performance logs.

Location Information

The Application optionally collects device location coordinates to power our smart place-tagging feature. When enabled, the app detects general stopping coordinates to help you easily tag and name where your spending occurred. This data is used strictly for app functionality and is not sold or shared with advertisers.


Third-Party Services (Google Firebase)

The Application uses Google Firebase for secure backend infrastructure, user authentication, and multi-device cloud synchronization (Firestore/Realtime Database). Data transmitted to Firebase is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.


Cookies and tracking technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, SDKs, or similar technologies to support functionality and analytics.


Account & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete your account and all associated financial records in two ways:

  • In-App Deletion: Open the My Spending Note app > go to Settings > tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your credentials and all synced spending entries from Firebase immediately.
  • External Request: If you have uninstalled the app, you can request manual data deletion by emailing the Service Provider at wanmuhdhatimzain@gmail.com with your registered email address.
